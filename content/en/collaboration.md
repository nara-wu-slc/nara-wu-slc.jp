## Collaborative Research

We welcome opportunities for funded collaborative research in fields related to our work. This option is suitable for medium- to long-term projects in which both parties contribute to the research. Please [contact us](contact) to discuss details.

## Academic Consultation

We also offer paid academic consultation on research and development challenges. This option is suitable for short-term technical consultation. Please [contact us](contact) to discuss details.
