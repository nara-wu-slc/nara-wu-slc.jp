## Collaborative Research

We welcome collaborative research with companies and other organizations in areas related to our work. Projects are arranged through Nara Women's University's institutional procedures and may involve fees. This option is suitable for medium- to long-term projects in which both parties contribute to the research. Please [contact us](contact) to discuss details.

## Academic Consultation

We also offer short-term academic consulting on research and development challenges through the university's institutional procedures. This option is suitable for focused technical consultation. Please [contact us](contact) to discuss details.
