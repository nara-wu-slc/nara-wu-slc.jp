## Speech Recognition
### Converting Speech Recognition Output into Written Style

<li>Automatically remove fillers such as “um” and “uh” during speech recognition</li>
<li>Fine-tune the large-scale Open Whisper-style Speech Model (OWSM)</li>

## Speech Synthesis
### Controlling Speech Synthesis with Musical Constraints
{{< cardpane >}}
{{< card >}}
    {{< video src="/videos/2025/sato.mp4" label="Japanese-language video introducing Yume Sato's speech synthesis research" >}}
{{< /card >}}
{{< card header="**Research Highlights**" footer="JSAI 2025 Annual Conference; Audio and Speech Processing Symposium" >}}
<ul>
<li>Partially control pitch during text-to-speech synthesis</li>
<li>Add symbols to the input text to specify the desired pitch</li>
<li>Learn the relationship between the symbols and pitch using the PJS Japanese singing-voice corpus</li>
<li>Next steps: expand the controllable pitch range and incorporate musical constraints other than pitch</li>
</ul>
